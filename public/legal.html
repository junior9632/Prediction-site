What GoalPredict is — and what it is not

GoalPredict publishes statistical analysis of one football betting market: Over 1.5 Goals. We are not a bookmaker. We do not accept bets, take deposits, process payments or hold customer funds. Nothing on this site is an invitation to bet, financial advice or a promise of profit. All bookmaker odds shown are quoted exactly as published by real bookmakers via our data provider and verified on our servers before publication.

No guarantees — ever

Football is uncertain by nature. Our predictions are probabilities derived from real data, and probabilities lose — regularly. Past performance, hit rates and streaks shown on the Analytics page never guarantee future results. On days when no combination of verified candidates meets our quality rules we publish NO QUALIFYING TICKET instead of forcing weak picks; that honesty is part of the product, not a malfunction. If you choose to bet, you do so at your own risk and you can lose your entire stake.

Responsible gambling — 18+

This site is intended for adults aged 18 or over (or the legal gambling age in your jurisdiction, if higher). Betting should only ever be entertainment, never a source of income and never a way to recover losses.

  • Only stake money you can afford to lose.
  • Set deposit, loss and time limits with your bookmaker — before you play.
  • Never chase losses. A losing day stays a losing day.
  • If gambling stops being fun, stop gambling.

If you or someone close to you has a gambling problem, free and confidential help is available: BeGambleAware, Gambling Therapy (international), GamCare. In Switzerland: SOS Spielsucht. Most licensed bookmakers also offer self-exclusion tools — use them if you need a break.

Privacy notice

We collect as little as possible:

  • Visitors: no account is required to read the site. We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics scripts.
  • Server logs: standard technical logs (IP address, request path, timestamp) are kept for security, rate limiting and abuse prevention, then rotated.
  • Accounts: if you register or sign in we store a username, e-mail address and a salted password hash (bcrypt) — never the password itself. A single session cookie is used for authentication; there are no marketing cookies.
  • Data sources: fixtures, odds, statistics and results come from API-Football. No visitor data is ever sent to the data provider.

To access or delete account data, contact the site operator at the address published on the homepage footer.
