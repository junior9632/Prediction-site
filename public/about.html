About

A football analysis service, not a betting shop

GoalPredict studies football fixtures and publishes a single daily prediction on one market. We are not a bookmaker, we do not take stakes, and we publish nothing we cannot trace back to real match data and a genuine bookmaker price.

One market

Every prediction is an Over 1.5 Goals line — nothing else, ever.

Real inputs

Fixtures and statistics from a professional football data provider.

Verified prices

Only prices quoted by real bookmakers are considered.

Traceable record

Every published day stays visible in results and history, wins and losses alike.

What we never do

No invented numbers

  • No fabricated fixtures, teams or markets.
  • No invented or "estimated" bookmaker odds.
  • No forced selections to reach a target.
  • No guarantee of profit, ever.

When no combination qualifies, the honest answer is that there is no qualifying ticket — and that is what the prediction page shows.
