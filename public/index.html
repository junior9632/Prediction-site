What is GoalPredict?

Football predictions built around data

GoalPredict analyses football fixtures using real match information, historical performance, goal trends and verified bookmaker prices to identify qualifying Over 1.5 Goals opportunities.

  • A goal model scores every fixture from expected goals, team and league Over 1.5 history.
  • Every candidate price is re-verified against a real bookmaker before publication.
  • Correlation and risk checks stop a leg that would duplicate or unbalance the ticket.
  • Every published prediction stays readable afterwards — with its result, win or loss.
