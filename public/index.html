What GoalPredict does

One market, mastered properly

Instead of scattering tips across dozens of markets, the engine concentrates on a single high-frequency question: will there be two goals or more?

Real odds, verified

Every price comes from a real bookmaker feed and passes 14 validation checks before it can be used. No invented numbers, no placeholder odds.

API-Football Pro

A model you can inspect

A Poisson goal model combines team scoring form, the league goal environment, head-to-head history and the market price into one confidence score — shown per match.

Transparent scoring

Rules that protect you

Correlation protection stops the same match pattern being stacked twice, and the combined odds must land inside 2.00–4.00. Nothing is ever padded to force a ticket.

Risk controlled

Honest when data is missing

If a price is stale, a fixture is uncertain or the feed is down, the candidate is rejected and the day is published as no qualifying ticket. We would rather publish nothing.

How the AI works

From raw fixture data to one daily ticket

Four steps, every single day — all of them on our servers, none of them improvised.

1

Ingest real data

Fixtures, results and Over 1.5 prices are synced from API-Football and stored with their verification timestamps.

2

Score every match

The Poisson model estimates P(2+ goals) from team form, league environment and head-to-head history.

3

Filter ruthlessly

Stale odds, low confidence, high risk, thin data and correlated legs are rejected — and counted in the diagnostics.

4

Publish by hand

A ticket is created only when the verified combination lands inside 2.00–4.00. Otherwise the day is honestly "no ticket".
